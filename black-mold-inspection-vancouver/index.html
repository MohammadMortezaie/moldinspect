Black-looking growth needs context, not panic.

Many molds can appear dark. The important questions are where moisture is coming from, what materials are affected, whether spores may be spreading, and what cleanup protocol is appropriate.

  • Dark staining on drywall, wood, or caulking
  • Leak, flood, or sewage backup history
  • Musty smell near affected area
  • Children, seniors, or sensitive occupants at home
